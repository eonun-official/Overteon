The moment saturation occurs, the topology engine wakes. It extracts only the harmonics the saturator has just created — sample-accurate, inside the oversampled domain — while your dry signal passes completely untouched.

This additive-only design solves phase interference and pre-ringing at the same time: the original signal is never subtracted from or re-phased, so there is no phase conflict to fix. You keep the support and translucency of the source and gain pure harmonic density — with effectively zero cost to your dynamics.

The sidechain EQ decides what the saturator hears; a mathematically exact inverse (de-emphasis) cancels the linear component, so what remains is distortion harmonics only — nothing added but the harmonics themselves, nothing taken away at all.
